Preface — Artificial Intelligence and the Service of Islamic Scholarship

Bismillah, Alhamdulillah, Salat & Salam upon RasulAllah.

AI has entered the madrasah, the university, the office of ifta', and the private study of talib al-'ilm with remarkable speed. Some advocate blanket rejection, others rely uncritically, accepting fluent answers as verified scholarship. Neither is sufficient. This Ummah is ummatan wasatan — justly balanced.

The Tool and the Scholar: AI should be treated as means, not independent religious authority. Al-wasa'il laha ahkam al-maqasid. Unlike search tools, AI can generate convincing citations that have no reliable basis.

Verification — Enduring Duty: Islamic scholarship has always required verification of transmitted knowledge. Generative AI may invent quotations, misattribute, conflate madhhabs, or supply incorrect page numbers. Allah commands us to verify.

Governing Principle: AI may assist scholarly work, but it must not become the authority upon which a scholarly conclusion rests. Barakah over virality.

Sample Prompts — 3 Real Examples From Book

SAMPLE 16 • (S) SOURCE-REQUIRED
16. Presence Check (Hadith)

Role: Disciplined research assistant. Never a mufti.
Task: Verify if this hadith wording exists in [BOOK NAME]. Access Declaration first. If no primary source inspectable: state "No primary sources inspectable. Recalled, not verified." Never invent hadith numbers, page numbers, or quotations. Label: Established / Not established. End with E. Check next: [exact editions to open].

→ Blocks invented hadith numbers — forces inspection of Shamela/Maktaba edition.
SAMPLE 31 • (S) FIQH
31. Four-Madhhab Position Map

Do not flatten madhhab differences. Present each madhhab separately with its own dalil, mu'tamad, and contemporary fatwa position. If no primary source inspectable: No primary sources inspectable. For personal case: This is information only, not a fatwa — route to qualified mufti. Separate layers: Primary text / Scholarly interpretation / AI inference. End with Evidence Ledger.

→ Prevents "all madhhabs say same" error — forces mu'tamad vs weak distinction.
SAMPLE 121 • (G) MUFTI INTAKE
121. Mufti Intake Interview

Help me prepare a question for a qualified mufti concerning [GENERAL TOPIC]. Privacy: Remove names, contacts, identity numbers. Do not assume anonymisation guarantees confidentiality. Do not upload confidential inquiry unless authorised. Safety: If immediate danger, abuse, self-harm — prioritize emergency services. Governing rule: Organise facts, identify missing info, prepare question. Must not manufacture facts, conceal uncertainty, issue personal fatwa, or imply AI answer has mufti authority.

→ Privacy & Safety Protocol p.193 — research aid, not fatwa issuer.
AUTHORITY SEPARATION — FOUR LAYERS (p.40-41): Layer 1 Qur'an Text — Layer 2 Sunna Text — Layer 3 Scholarly Interpretation — Layer 4 AI Inference. Every prompt ends with "E. Check next: [exact primary texts, editions, pages that must still be opened]" + Verification Protocol 13-check (pp.243-245) + Stop Rule: If essential evidence missing, leave empty, mark unresolved, do not invent conclusion.
